Lift Operator

Keep a growing hotel moving — then automate the chaos.

How to Play

Send lifts to floors, collect guests, and deliver them before they lose patience. Spend Credits between rounds, then use the Automation Dock and Workshop to manage larger fleets.

Some rounds add special traffic: checkout guests need Ground, VIPs need priority, and rooftop demand changes suddenly.
